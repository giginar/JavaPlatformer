# DeepDive Drift Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

DeepDive Drift does not collect personal data, share data with third parties, or transmit data to a server. The game contains no advertising, user accounts, analytics, online payments, or location tracking.

The game stores only the high score and music, sound, and visual-effect preferences locally on the device. This information does not leave the device. It can be removed by clearing the app's data in Android settings or uninstalling the app.

This policy will be updated if a new online feature or third-party service that processes data is added to the application.
