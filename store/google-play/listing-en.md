## Full description

Protect your oxygen as the abyss pulls you down, dodge hostile sea creatures, and fight back with your harpoon.

DeepDive Drift is an offline underwater survival game that is easy to learn and increasingly hard to master. Hold to swim, release to sink, and hunt incoming threats. Collect oxygen tanks, build a score combo, and choose a diver upgrade whenever you reach a new depth level.

Features:

- Fast, replayable survival runs
- Sea creatures with distinct movement and attack patterns
- Rapid Fire, Piercing, Air Recycler, Pressurized Tanks, and Hydro Fins upgrades
- A multi-stage Abyss Leviathan boss battle
- A locally saved high score
- Separate options for screen shake and flashing effects
- No ads, no account, and fully offline play
