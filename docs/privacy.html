Local game data

The game stores gameplay data locally on the device. This includes Pressure Pearls, equipment and suit progression, achievements, high score and run-completion records, difficulty and challenge choices, and audio, display, screen-shake, and flashing-effect settings. Current run state is held in memory while a run is active.

The game has no player account, publisher-operated server, cloud save, real-money purchase, or social or user-generated-content feature. The Android build disables application backup. The game code does not transmit this local gameplay data to the publisher. Players can remove it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Advertising and consent processing on Android

The Android build includes Google Mobile Ads Next-Gen and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Core gameplay can run without an internet connection, but advertising and consent operations require network access.

Google states that the Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares data for advertising, analytics, and fraud-prevention purposes, including:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • product interactions, such as app launches, taps, and video views;
  • diagnostic information, such as app-launch time, hang rate, and energy use; and
  • device and account identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID, App Set ID, and other applicable identifiers.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK contributes the Advertising ID permission. Android and Google advertising settings can limit, reset, or delete that identifier, and age-related or regional privacy signals may prevent its transmission. The final audience configuration has not yet been selected.

UMP requests updated consent information when the app starts, presents a consent form when Google indicates one is required, and makes privacy options available again when required. The game requests ads only when UMP reports that ads may be requested. The messages and choices shown depend on the user's region, Google's Privacy & Messaging configuration, and the user's response.

Google states that Mobile Ads data is encrypted in transit using TLS. Google controls its processing and retention of advertising and consent data. See Google's privacy policy, Google's advertising policies, and the Google Mobile Ads SDK disclosure.

Other services

The game does not include a standalone analytics, publisher crash-reporting, account, billing, payment, cloud-save, or precise-location SDK. Google Mobile Ads nevertheless uses interaction and diagnostic data for the purposes above. SDK crash reporting is disabled in the game's Mobile Ads configuration.

Children and age treatment

The intended target age groups have not been selected. The app has not yet been configured as child-directed or for users under the age of consent. If the selected audience includes children, the advertising configuration and this policy must be reviewed and updated before release.

Questions and deletion requests

There is no publisher-held account or server copy of local game data. Players can delete local game data through Android's app-storage controls or by uninstalling the app. Questions concerning publisher-controlled data may be sent to ykucukcinar@gmail.com. Google's privacy controls and policies govern data processed by Google.

Changes

This policy must be reviewed whenever the app's audience, countries, advertising configuration, SDK versions, or data practices change.
